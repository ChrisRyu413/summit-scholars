Select each stage to review the support included.

01BUILD THE FOUNDATIONExperience & Clinical Exposure

We build experiences that demonstrate sustained understanding of dentistry—not activities completed only to check a box.

  • Shadowing & Clinical Experience StrategyPlan target hours, settings, and specialties for balanced clinical exposure
  • Volunteer Experience ManagementConnect service with the student's values and application story
  • Outreach & Opportunity DevelopmentResearch organizations, prepare personalized outreach, and manage follow-ups
  • Progress TrackingDocument hours, roles, outcomes, and lessons throughout the process
02FIND THE RIGHT FITSchool Selection & Application Strategy

We build a realistic, balanced strategy based on the student's academic profile and goals—not rankings alone.

  • Build the School ListCreate a balanced list reflecting goals and competitiveness
  • Verify PrerequisitesReview required courses, credits, tests, and school-specific policies
  • Assess CompetitivenessEvaluate GPA, DAT, experiences, and each school's profile together
  • Application TimelinePlan preparation and submission around rolling admissions
03SHAPE YOUR STORYPersonal Statement Development

We uncover the student's own answer to 'Why dentistry?' and shape it into a clear narrative connecting experience, values, and goals.

  • Personal Statement PlanningDefine the central message and differentiating qualities
  • Narrative StructureConnect experiences, motivation, growth, and future goals
  • Drafting GuidanceDevelop structure and language while preserving the student's voice
  • Revision & Final DraftRefine content, flow, and expression through structured revisions
04EXECUTE WITH PRECISIONAADSAS & Supplemental Applications

We organize a process with many sections and deadlines so every submission is accurate, complete, and aligned around one message.

  • AADSAS ReviewCheck accuracy and consistency across the complete application
  • Activities & ExperiencesDescribe roles, contributions, growth, and impact clearly
  • Recommendation LettersPlan recommenders, request timing, and submission tracking
  • Supplemental Essay ReviewDevelop responses tailored to each school's values and questions
  • Submission & Deadline ManagementTrack deadlines, additional requests, and application status
05FINISH STRONGInterviews & Post-Acceptance Decisions

From interview invitations through the final decision, we prepare the right message and decision criteria for each situation.

  • School-Specific Interview PreparationPrepare for each school's format, values, and likely questions
  • Mock InterviewImprove answers through realistic practice and specific feedback
  • Update Letter & Letter of IntentCommunicate new achievements and continued interest strategically
  • Waitlist StrategyPlan follow-up based on each school's policies and situation
  • Final School DecisionCompare education, cost, environment, and career goals
